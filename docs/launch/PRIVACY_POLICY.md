# CareerOS Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 16 September 2026**

This is the user-facing privacy policy for the CareerOS mobile app. It describes what CareerOS
actually does today — every statement below matches the shipped implementation (the engineering
detail behind it is in `PRIVACY.md` in the project repository).

Publish this page at a public URL before submitting to Google Play (Play requires a reachable
privacy policy link), and put the same link in the app listing and in Settings → About.

## What we collect

**Account.** Your email address and password (stored only as a salted hash — we never store or see
your actual password).

**Profile you choose to give us.** Name, professional title, location, years of experience,
education, field of study.

**Career documents.** When you upload a CV for analysis, we extract the text from it and store that
text. The original file is not kept. CV text is used only to produce your own analysis results.

**Your activity in the app.** Applications you track and their stages, opportunities you save,
practice tests and interview sessions you complete, STAR stories you write, and companies you follow.

**Interview practice recordings.** If you record an answer during interview practice, the audio is
stored so you can play it back. You can delete it.

**Connected mailboxes (optional, off by default).** If you ever connect Gmail or Outlook, we request
read-only access for one purpose: to spot recruitment emails and suggest a stage update for an
application you already track. We read the message in memory to classify it; **we never store the
email body**. We keep only the sender, subject line, received date, the matched application, the
suggested stage, a confidence score and a short reason. Access tokens are encrypted. You can
disconnect at any time, which stops all further processing. CareerOS works fully without ever
connecting a mailbox.

**Technical data.** Standard server logs needed to run and secure the service. Logs never contain
passwords, tokens, email bodies, CV text or documents.

## What we do not do

- We do **not** sell your personal data.
- We do **not** send your CV, documents, recordings or email content to any third-party AI service.
  Matching, scoring and classification run on our own servers with deterministic rules.
- We do **not** use your inbox for advertising.
- We do **not** change an application's stage without your explicit confirmation.

## Advertising

CareerOS may show ads to support the free tier. Ads never appear during aptitude tests, mock
interviews, results review, application stage updates, email-tracking review, sign-up, sign-in or
settings. Where required by law, you are asked for consent before personalised ads, and you can
change that choice at any time in Settings → Ads & Privacy. Your CV, application history, email
content and other private career data are never used to target ads.

## Where your data is stored

On our servers (currently hosted with Render, in the EU region) and in the app on your device.
Data is encrypted in transit with HTTPS.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Delete an item (a CV, a recording, a saved
opportunity) and it is removed. Delete your account from Settings and we remove your personal data;
backups roll off on their normal cycle.

## Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data. Most of this is available in the app: Settings
→ Account. For anything else, contact <YOUR CONTACT EMAIL> and we will respond within 30 days. If
you are in an area covered by the GDPR or the Nigeria Data Protection Act, you also have the right
to object to processing and to complain to your data protection authority.

## Children

CareerOS is intended for people aged 16 and over and is not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and notify you in the app before
the change takes effect.
